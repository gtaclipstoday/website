GIVEAWAY · NOV 19 – DEC 24, 2026

Win a limited GTA 6 PS5 controller

To celebrate the release of GTA 6 we're giving away an original PlayStation 5 DualSense controller in the limited GTA VI design (black) – brand new and sealed in its original box, perfect for collectors. The community picks the winner – revealed on Christmas Eve.

How it works

  1. Play GTA 6 and record your craziest, funniest or most insane moments - new features, NPC reactions, fails, stunts, everything counts.
  2. Send it to us - two ways: on PS5 as a PSN message to GTA_Clips_Today (Create button → Share → send to a player, no friend request needed), or upload it right on gtaclipstoday.com (no account needed). Send as many clips as you want - every clip is a new chance!
  3. Every week we pick 4 of the best clips and you vote for the Clip of the Week on our YouTube channel and in our Instagram story.
  4. In the final (Dec 19–24) the four weekly winners go head to head – the community votes again and the winner is revealed on Christmas Eve.

Follow @gtaclipstoday on Instagram (required to win) and subscribe on YouTube so you don't miss the votes.

Bonus prize: €20 PSN credit raffle

Every clip of yours that makes it into one of our videos = 1 ticket (YouTube, Shorts, TikTok or Instagram). On Christmas Eve we draw one ticket - the winner gets a €20 PlayStation Store credit. More clips in our videos = better chances!

2. Prize

One (1) original PlayStation 5 DualSense wireless controller in the limited GTA VI design, black, brand new and sealed in its original packaging. The prize cannot be exchanged for cash or another item. If the prize cannot be delivered for reasons beyond our control, we may replace it with an item of similar value.

3. Who can take part

  • Anyone aged 16 or older. Participants under 18 need the consent of a parent or legal guardian, who must confirm it if they win.
  • Participants worldwide, except residents of countries to which shipping is prohibited by law or sanctions (for example Russia, Belarus, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba).
  • Excluded: the organizer and his family members.
  • To be eligible to win, you must follow @gtaclipstoday on Instagram at the time the winner is determined.

4. How to enter

  • Send a clip between November 19, 2026, 00:00 CET and December 17, 2026, 23:59 CET, either as a PlayStation Network message to our PSN account GTA_Clips_Today or with the upload form on gtaclipstoday.com. Taking part is free - no purchase needed.
  • The clip must be gameplay of Grand Theft Auto VI, recorded by yourself during the giveaway period. Clips from other people, other games, edited compilations or re-uploads are not allowed.
  • You may send several clips. Each clip takes part in the week in which we received it. A person can win the final only once; if one person wins several weeks, the runner-up of the later week moves into the final.
  • By sending a clip you accept our Terms for submissions, including that we may publish the clip with your PSN Online ID or the gamertag you entered as credit.

5. How the winner is determined

  • Weekly: after each week we select 4 eligible clips (checking the rules above and quality). The community votes for the Clip of the Week in a poll on our YouTube channel and in a poll in our Instagram story, each open for about 24 hours.
  • Counting: both platforms count equally. Each clip's result is the average of its percentage of votes on YouTube and on Instagram. On a tie, the higher total number of votes wins. If one poll cannot be held for technical reasons, the other poll decides alone.
  • Final: the four weekly winners compete in a final vote from December 19, 2026 to December 24, 2026, 18:00 CET. On YouTube one poll is open for the whole period; on Instagram a new story poll is posted every day and the votes of all days are added up. The result per clip is calculated as above. The clip with the best result wins the prize.
  • Fair play: buying votes, using bots or fake accounts, or any other manipulation is not allowed. In such cases we may disqualify the clip concerned. We may also exclude clips that break these rules or our Terms.

5a. Bonus prize "€20 PSN credit raffle"

  • Prize: one digital PlayStation Store credit code worth €20 (or the closest available amount in the winner's country store, as PSN credit only works in its own region). No cash alternative.
  • Tickets: every clip submitted during the giveaway period via PSN or our upload form that is published in at least one of our videos (YouTube, Shorts, TikTok or Instagram) between November 19 and December 23, 2026 earns its submitter (PSN Online ID or gamertag as credited) one ticket. Each clip counts once, no matter how many videos it appears in. Clips that are not published earn no ticket.
  • Draw: on December 24, 2026 one ticket is drawn at random from all tickets; its owner wins. The same eligibility rules, fair-play rules and notification rules as for the main prize apply. One person can win both prizes.
  • The selection of clips for our videos is our editorial decision; there is no claim to have a clip published.

6. Winner notification and delivery

  • We announce the winner by PSN Online ID or gamertag on December 24, 2026 in our videos/posts. We contact the winner via PSN message (PSN entries) or at the email address entered in the upload form. If we have no way to reach the winner, the winner must contact us at contact@gtaclipstoday.com within 7 days.
  • The winner has 7 days to reply with a shipping address. Otherwise the prize goes to the clip with the next-best result in the final.
  • On request, the winner must show that they recorded the clip themselves (for example the original file in the PS5 media gallery).
  • We ship as an insured parcel at our cost within 3 working days after receiving the address (because of the holidays, usually from December 28). Any customs duties, import taxes or fees in the winner's country are paid by the winner.

7. Data protection

We process your PSN Online ID or gamertag, your clip and - if you entered one in the upload form - your email address to run the giveaway and publish the clips (see our Privacy Policy). Your email address is only used to contact you if you win and is never published. The winner's name and shipping address are used only to send the prize and deleted after delivery, unless the law requires us to keep them. The polls run on YouTube and Instagram under their own privacy policies.

8. Other

We may end or change the giveaway early only if it cannot be carried out properly for important reasons (for example technical problems or legal requirements) – in that case we will announce it on our channels. German law applies, without prejudice to mandatory consumer protection laws of your country of residence.
