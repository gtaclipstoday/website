Other ways: upload from your phone or PC

Xbox player, long recording or no PSN? Let your console send clips to your phone automatically, then upload them with our form. Menu names can differ a little depending on your system version.

PlayStation 5 (via phone)

Once: turn on auto-upload

  1. On your PS5 go to Settings → Captures and Broadcasts → Auto-Upload and switch it on.
  2. Install the PlayStation App on your phone and sign in with the same account.

Every time

  1. Something crazy happened? Press the Create button and choose Save recent gameplay.
  2. Open the PlayStation App, find your Captures and save the clip to your phone.
  3. Open our submit form on your phone and upload it.

Long recording that doesn't show up in the app? Copy it to a USB stick instead: Media Gallery → select the video → … → Copy to USB drive, then upload it from a PC.

Xbox Series X|S

Once: turn on auto-upload

  1. On your Xbox go to Settings → Preferences → Capture & share and turn on Automatically upload.
  2. Install the Xbox App on your phone and sign in with the same account.

Every time

  1. Press the Share button on your controller to save what just happened.
  2. In the Xbox App open your Captures and save the clip to your phone.
  3. Upload it with our submit form.

FAQ

Do I get paid?

No, submissions are unpaid. In return you get credited on screen and in the video description, and thousands of people get to see your moment.

Which name is shown?

Only the name or gamertag you type into the form. We never show your email address or real name.

Why do I have to sign in with Google?

Google requires a sign-in for file uploads in Google Forms. We only see the email address of the account, to contact you if needed.

How long until my clip is online?

Usually within a few days. Not every clip makes it in. We pick the best moments for each video.
