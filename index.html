    Getting the clip off your console

    PlayStation 5 (to your phone)

    Once: turn on auto-upload

    1. On your PS5 go to Settings → Captures and Broadcasts → Auto-Upload and switch it on.
    2. Install the PlayStation App on your phone and sign in with the same account.

    Every time

    1. Press the Create button and choose Save recent gameplay.
    2. In the PlayStation App open your Captures and save the clip to your phone.
    3. Upload it with our upload form.

    Long recording that doesn't show up in the app? Copy it to a USB stick (Media Gallery → select the video → … → Copy to USB drive) and upload it from a PC.

    Xbox Series X|S

    Once: turn on auto-upload

    1. On your Xbox go to Settings → Preferences → Capture & share and turn on Automatically upload.
    2. Install the Xbox App on your phone and sign in with the same account.

    Every time

    1. Press the Share button on your controller to save what just happened.
    2. In the Xbox App open your Captures and save the clip to your phone.
    3. Upload it with our upload form.

    Menu names can differ a little depending on your system version.

    FAQ

    Do I get paid?

    No, submissions are unpaid. In return you get credited on screen and in the video description, and thousands of people get to see your moment.

    Which name is shown?

    The name or gamertag you type into the form, or your PSN Online ID if you send the clip from your PS5. We never show your email address or real name.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Our upload form works without any account or email address - just your gamertag and the video. On PS5 you can also send the clip as a PSN message to GTA_Clips_Today.

    How long until my clip is online?

    Usually within a few days. Not every clip makes it in. We pick the best moments for each video, so follow us to catch yours.
