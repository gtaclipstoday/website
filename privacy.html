2. This website

This website is a static page hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the page and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising, and all fonts are served from this website itself. Because nothing is stored on or read from your device when you just visit this website, there is no cookie banner. Links to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Discord are plain links; those services only receive data once you click them.

3. Submitting a clip with our upload form

When you use the upload form on gtaclipstoday.com/upload.html, we receive:

  • the video files you upload or the links you paste (videos may contain your voice or in-game name),
  • the gamertag you enter (this is the name we show as credit),
  • optional information: your email address (only to contact you if you win the giveaway, never published), your channel or social handle, a description and the clip category,
  • your confirmation that you recorded the clip yourself and accept our Terms,
  • the country your connection comes from (derived from your IP address by our hosting provider).

No account and no email address are needed. We use this data to review your clip, edit it, publish it with credit and, if you gave us a handle, to tag you. Legal basis: our agreement under the Terms (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

The upload is handled by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) as our processor: files are stored in Cloudflare R2 storage only until our system has downloaded them to our own computer, then deleted there (usually within an hour). To protect the form from spam bots we use Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks technical browser and connection data (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our interest in a spam-free service). Turnstile is only loaded once you start using the upload form; simply visiting this website does not load it. It is technically necessary to provide the upload you request (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

3a. Submitting a clip with the Google Form

When you submit a clip, we receive:

  • the email address of the Google account you sign in with,
  • the name or gamertag you enter (this is the name we show as credit),
  • optional information: social media handle and a description of the clip,
  • your confirmation that you allow us to use the clip,
  • the video files you upload (which may contain your voice or in-game name).

We use this data to review your clip, edit it, publish it with credit and contact you about it. Legal basis: our agreement under the Terms that you accept when submitting (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in running the channel (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The form and uploads are provided by Google (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). Uploaded files are stored in our Google Drive only until our system has downloaded them to our own computer; after that they are deleted from Google Drive.

4. Submitting by email

If you email us a clip or a link, we process your email address, the content of your email and the attached or linked video for the same purposes as described above.

4a. Submitting a clip via PlayStation Network

If you send a clip as a PlayStation Network message to our PSN account GTA_Clips_Today, we receive your PSN Online ID, the clip and any message text. We use them for the same purposes as described above and show your PSN Online ID as credit. The messaging service is provided by Sony Interactive Entertainment; its own privacy policy applies to the use of PlayStation Network.

4b. Our Discord server

If you join our Discord server or post a clip in it, we see your Discord username, your messages and the files you post. We use clips posted in the submission channel for the same purposes as described above and show your Discord name or the gamertag you give us as credit. Discord is provided by Discord Inc. (USA); its own privacy policy applies. Our server sends automatic notices about new videos; it does not collect any further data about you.

5. Publication

Selected clips are published on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook together with the name or gamertag you gave us. Your email address is never published. Once published, the content is also subject to the privacy policies of those platforms.

6. Google API Services and YouTube API Services

Our internal tool "GTA Clips Pipeline" uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API) as well as the Google Forms API and the Google Drive API, exclusively for the GTA Clips Today Google account: to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel, to set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, to read our own channel's statistics, to read form submissions, and to download and then delete uploaded files. It does not access data of any other Google or YouTube user and it is not offered to other users.

By using YouTube API Services, this tool is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's processing of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The data we receive (video IDs, upload status and our own channel statistics) is stored only on our own computer and used only to manage our channel. Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

GTA Clips Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell data, show ads based on it or share it with third parties.

7. How long we keep data

Submission data and original files are kept as long as we need them for our videos. Published videos stay online as described in our Terms. Your email address and other non-public submission data can be deleted on request.

8. Your rights

Within the limits of the law, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing and to data portability. Just email us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
